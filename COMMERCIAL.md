# Commercial Lians

The Apache-licensed Community software is the adoption and trust layer. Lians
customers pay for the operated system, private product surface, implementation,
evidence delivery, and accountability required to use it in consequential
workflows.

Buying Lians is not a fee for downloading this repository. It adds a defined
workflow, an owned deployment boundary, measurable acceptance criteria, and a
party responsible for delivery and support.

## Current entry offers

| Offer | Scope | Price |
|---|---|---:|
| Decision Evidence Diagnostic | Five business days to map one blocked evidence request and recommend stop, remediate, proof, or design-partner action | $2,500 fixed |
| Proof Sprint | Two weeks to test one qualified workflow in a production-representative environment | $7,500 fixed |
| Design Partnership | Six weeks to instrument and validate one protected workflow with a named sponsor and decision date | $20,000 fixed |
| Annual Infrastructure | Operated or supported infrastructure after repeatable value is demonstrated | Target $60,000 to $75,000 ACV |

Final pricing depends on deployment boundary, workflow count, evidence volume,
retention, integrations, security obligations, support, and service levels. A
signed agreement is the source of truth.

## What a commercial agreement can add

- Lians Cloud, dedicated, customer-cloud, on-premises, or air-gapped delivery;
- private control-plane and enterprise modules that are available for the
  contracted release;
- deployment, upgrades, monitoring, backups, restore rehearsals, and incident
  response;
- SSO, identity, key-management, SIEM, workflow, and data-source integration;
- evidence-requirement mapping, signed deliverables, and release acceptance;
- private connectors and customer-specific evaluation or policy packs;
- named support, response targets, and service-level commitments; and
- security and procurement support, including BAA or DPA work where applicable.

Lians provides software and technical controls, not legal advice,
certification, or a guarantee that a customer's deployment is compliant.
