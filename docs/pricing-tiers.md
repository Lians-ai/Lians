# Lians packaging and pricing

Date: August 11, 2026
Status: Canonical commercial source of truth

## Packaging principle

Lians Community is the public, Apache-licensed foundation. It gives developers
portable evidence formats, SDKs, verifiers, local operation, and a self-hosted
path they can inspect independently.

Commercial Lians is the full operated experience. Customers buy a defined
workflow, private product capabilities available for the contracted release,
deployment ownership, integrations, evidence delivery, security work, support,
and service commitments. They are not paying merely for permission to download
the Community repository.

The source boundary is defined in [../OPEN_CORE.md](../OPEN_CORE.md).

## Community: $0

- Apache-licensed source published in the public repository
- Local and self-hosted deployment
- Public evidence formats and offline verification
- SDKs, MCP, documented ingestion interfaces, and framework hooks
- Point-in-time memory and decision-evidence workflows
- Public documentation, demos, and reproducible benchmarks
- Community issue support with no response-time commitment

Community operators own deployment, upgrades, backups, security configuration,
availability, capacity, incident response, and regulatory assessment.

## Lians Cloud for developers and teams

| Plan | Monthly price | Included hosted capabilities |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | Token-reduced context, 10K writes and recalls, point-in-time memory, API keys, and playground |
| Starter | $15 | 100K writes, domain adapters, audit history, and lineage |
| Growth | $69 | Governance review, graph, conflicts, webhooks, and reports |
| Pro | $199 | Adaptive recall, experience learning, backtest checks, metrics, and priority support |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, governed access, KMS, private or air-gapped deployment, security review, and SLA |

Lians-operated services enforce plan scopes on the server. The web interface
mirrors those permissions for clarity, but hiding a button is never treated as
authorization. Self-hosted Community deployments are administered by their
operator and do not call Lians for a license check.

## Decision Evidence Diagnostic: $2,500 fixed

Five business days for one synthetic or sanitized fraud, identity, AML,
underwriting, risk, healthcare, legal, or other consequential agent workflow.

Includes:

- evidence-requirement map;
- historical map of facts, policy, model, identity, permissions, approvals, and
  tools;
- one point-in-time reconstruction;
- one controlled change to data, policy, model, permission, provenance, or
  timing;
- evidence-gap report; and
- a stop, remediate, Proof Sprint, or design-partner recommendation.

## Proof Sprint: $7,500 fixed

Two weeks for a workflow that passes diagnostic qualification and needs bounded
production-representative integration proof.

The sprint has explicit acceptance criteria, a named owner, a deployment
boundary, and a commercial decision date. Fifty percent upfront is the default
unless the signed order form says otherwise.

## Design Partnership: $20,000 fixed

Six weeks for one protected workflow with a named sponsor, measurable success
criteria, access to the required technical environment, and an agreed decision
date.

## Annual Infrastructure: $60,000 to $75,000 target ACV

Annual scope is proposed after repeatable operational value is demonstrated.
Depending on availability and contract, it may include:

- Lians Cloud, a dedicated environment, customer cloud, private VPC, on-premises,
  or air-gapped delivery;
- private control-plane and enterprise modules;
- managed upgrades, monitoring, backups, restore rehearsals, and incident
  response;
- SSO, identity, KMS, SIEM, data-source, workflow, and policy integrations;
- managed evidence retention and delivery;
- customer-specific connectors, evaluation packs, or deployment automation;
- named support and response targets; and
- security, procurement, BAA, DPA, or architecture-review work where applicable.

## Commercial boundaries

- Availability varies by product maturity and contracted release.
- Final scope, contracting entity, payment terms, data responsibilities,
  security boundary, and start date belong in a signed agreement.
- Lians does not certify compliance, legal sufficiency, model quality, security,
  or production readiness.
- No free production integration is included.
- No Proof Sprint proposal is issued before the workflow passes qualification.
- Customer data, configurations, evaluation cases, deployment automation, and
  deliverables remain in private systems and repositories.
