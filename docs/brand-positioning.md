# Lians brand and category position

## The category

Lians is the **system of record and control for consequential AI decisions**.

Do not lead with "AI infrastructure," "agent memory," or "observability." Those
describe implementation surfaces and crowded comparison sets. Lians owns the
larger outcome: making AI decisions answerable across models, agents, providers,
and time.

## The promise

> Make every AI decision answerable.

An answerable decision can show:

1. **What did it know?** Point-in-time memory, inputs, and provenance.
2. **Why did it act?** The material evidence boundary and decision path.
3. **Who authorized it?** Identity, policy, information barriers, and approvals.
4. **What changed next?** Later corrections, exposed decisions, remediation, and closure.

The compact brand line is:

> Memory. Authority. Evidence. Action.

## The product system

| Job | Product primitive | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Record what mattered | Universal Recorder | One neutral event and memory record across runtimes |
| Prove the boundary | Decision Receipt | Portable, independently inspectable proof |
| Govern the action | Authority Gate | Exact identity and policy bound before release |
| Learn from change | Impact Intelligence | Prioritized blast radius without invented causality |

Memory Studio is the developer and operator surface for the first job. The
Decision System connects all four jobs into one proof loop.

## Who it is for

- **Individual developers** get local-first memory, one-command MCP, SDKs,
  deterministic evaluations, progressive recall, and a visual Memory Studio.
- **Platform teams** get one control surface across model and agent providers.
- **Risk, compliance, security, and operations teams** get portable receipts,
  explicit completeness, historical reconstruction, information barriers, and
  remediation evidence.

## Proof before adjectives

The brand should demonstrate, not merely claim. Prefer a receipt someone can
verify, an incident someone can investigate, a benchmark someone can rerun, or
a policy someone can inspect over words such as "enterprise-grade," "trusted,"
or "powerful."

Primary proof assets:

- the canonical lending incident and independently verifiable Decision Receipt;
- Track E0, the deterministic lookahead-bias explorer;
- RIAD-1 reconstruction and completeness gates;
- the live Memory Studio and governed lifecycle controls;
- the official MCP Registry listing and local-first quickstart.

## Voice

Be direct, exact, and consequential. Use short declarative sentences. Name the
recorded boundary and disclose what Lians cannot prove. Confidence comes from
specificity, not hype.

Use:

- "Make every AI decision answerable."
- "The record AI cannot rewrite after the fact."
- "Know what it knew before the outcome was known."
- "One portable record across every model, agent, and provider."
- "Find every decision at risk when truth changes."

Avoid:

- "A better memory layer."
- "All-in-one AI infrastructure."
- "Revolutionary" or "game-changing."
- claims of hidden chain-of-thought access;
- claims of universal deterministic replay;
- generic trust language without an inspectable artifact.

## One-sentence versions

**Company:** Lians makes consequential AI decisions answerable by preserving
what the system knew, why it acted, who authorized it, and what changed next.

**Developer:** Add durable, governed memory and verifiable decision receipts to
any agent without giving one model provider control of the record.

**Enterprise:** Lians is the neutral system of record and control for AI
decisions across models, agents, providers, and regulated workflows.

**Repository description:** Make every AI decision answerable. Open-source
system of record and control for consequential AI: memory, authority, evidence,
and impact.
