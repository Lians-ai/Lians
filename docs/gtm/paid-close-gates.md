# Paid design-partnership close gates

Do not record a design partnership as won until every gate below has objective
evidence.

1. **Named workflow:** one consequential AI workflow and technical owner.
2. **Named buyer:** person authorized to approve the $4,500 engagement.
3. **Commercial acceptance:** written confirmation of the fixed price and
   two-week scope.
4. **Signed agreement:** order form signed by both legal entities.
5. **Invoice issued:** $2,250 kickoff invoice sent to the verified billing
   contact.
6. **Payment confirmed:** cleared funds or authoritative processor confirmation.
7. **Kickoff scheduled:** only after gates 1–6 are complete.

The following do **not** satisfy these gates: a free pilot, unpaid evaluation,
integration interest, accelerator acceptance, partner-program membership,
customer introduction, verbal enthusiasm, unsigned scope, pending invoice, or
promised payment.

## Evidence to retain

- final signed order form and incorporated scope;
- customer legal name and billing contact;
- invoice number and issue date;
- payment confirmation identifier and date;
- workflow name, technical owner, and kickoff date.
