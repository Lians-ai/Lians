## Allowed outcome values

- `buyer-order-form`: buyer accepted the paid scope and supplied the required
  order-form inputs.
- `qualified-decision`: named workflow, owner, budget path, paid acceptance, and
  a dated decision involving the approver.
- `funded-introduction`: named external buyer and introduction sent with the
  $4,500 framing.
- `owner-action`: accountable person accepted a specific action and date, but
  budget or approver is not yet confirmed.
- `no-fit`: explicit reason recorded.
- `exploratory`: interest without the qualification evidence above.
- `no-show`: attendee did not join.
