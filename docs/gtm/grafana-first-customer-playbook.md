# Lians first paying customer playbook

Updated: July 27, 2026

## The sale

Do not begin by asking a company to buy a new governance platform. Sell a small,
fixed outcome that uses infrastructure it already has.

**Paid Design Partnership: AI Evidence Readiness Sprint — $4,500 fixed**

- Two weeks
- One production-like or sanitized AI workflow
- One existing OpenTelemetry trace path
- Alloy fan-out into Lians without replacing Grafana
- Reconstruction of the memory, sources, policies, permissions, tool results,
  and approvals behind one consequential decision
- Evidence-gap map, working dashboard, verifiable receipt, and production plan
- This is paid implementation work, not a free pilot, evaluation, or proof of concept
- The fee is credited toward a longer paid design partnership if signed within 30 days

Commercial terms: 50% to start and 50% on delivery. No production credentials
are required for the sprint. The customer may use synthetic or sanitized data.

## Why this is worth paying for

The buyer is not paying for another dashboard. The buyer receives an answer to
a concrete risk question:

> If this AI decision is challenged six months from now, can we prove exactly
> what the system knew, which policy applied, and what changed afterward?

Grafana continues to show operational behavior. Lians creates the
decision-time evidence record and exposes missing sources, broken references,
conflicting or stale memories, and downstream impact.

## Twenty-minute call

1. Ask for one decision the company would struggle to explain after its data or
   policies change.
2. Map the decision ID to its trace ID.
3. Ask where retrieved documents, memory, policy versions, permissions, and
   approvals live.
4. Identify which of those inputs can be reproduced as-of the decision time.
5. Show the Lians Grafana integration and one deliberately broken evidence chain.
6. Offer the fixed sprint and ask directly: “If we can deliver this in two
   weeks using sanitized data, can you approve the $4,500 sprint?”

## Rules

- Contact named people only after verifying their current role.
- Personalize from public technical evidence; never imply a private system detail.
- Do not claim Lians is listed in the Grafana catalog until approval is complete.
- Do not offer an unpaid pilot. A short qualification call may be complimentary,
  but it includes no implementation, integration, analysis, or deliverable.
- Stop sequences after three relevant touches.
- Track consent, replies, objections, next action, and payment status.

## Payment milestone

scope and a paid **$2,250 kickoff invoice**. Product access follows payment.
