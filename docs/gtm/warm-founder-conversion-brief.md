# Warm founder conversion brief

Use this for the Playbook, Omeca, and Protum introductions. The goal is to discover a real workflow and buyer—not to force a pitch into a peer conversation.

## Five-minute structure

1. Ask what the product does autonomously today.
2. Identify one consequential decision the team would struggle to reconstruct later.
3. Ask what changes after the decision: source data, policies, prompts, models, permissions, or downstream systems.
4. Ask who owns auditability, disputes, model risk, or customer trust for that workflow.
5. If the pain is concrete, propose the paid sprint. Otherwise, ask for one relevant customer or operator introduction.

## Qualification questions

- Which AI decisions or actions reach production users today?
- What happens when a source document, transaction, policy, or memory changes after an action?
- Can the team reproduce exactly what the agent knew and which version it relied on?
- Has a customer, regulator, auditor, or internal risk owner asked for this evidence?
- Is there one sanitized workflow available for a two-week implementation?
- Who can approve a $4,500 fixed-scope paid design partnership?
- Is there a deadline or customer event that makes the work urgent?

## Paid offer

**Paid Design Partnership: AI Evidence Readiness Sprint — $4,500 fixed**

- 50% at kickoff and 50% at delivery;
- one sanitized production-representative workflow;
- source, memory, model, policy, tool-call, approval, and effect lineage;
- point-in-time reconstruction;
- duplicate, conflict, staleness, source, and broken-reference checks;
- OpenTelemetry/Grafana correlation where applicable;
- implementation and evidence-gap report.

## Account-specific angles

### Playbook

Focus on transaction categorization, financial reporting, close management, and finance-team operations. The demonstration should reconstruct one AI-produced categorization or report item after its underlying transaction, policy, or mapping has changed.

Primary ask: a paid design partnership on one sanitized finance workflow.
Fallback ask: an introduction to one design partner that has requested stronger auditability.

### Omeca

Position Lians as the evidence substrate beneath Omeca's agent identity and decision controls. Omeca answers who was authorized; Lians preserves what that agent knew, relied on, and affected.

Primary ask: choose one joint workflow and identify which party can sponsor the sprint.
Fallback ask: a co-sell or mutual-reference experiment with one regulated enterprise.

### Protum

Position Lians as temporal evidence and replay beneath TrustOps and assurance controls. Avoid duplicating governance dashboards; focus on immutable decision-time evidence and dependency impact.

Primary ask: identify one regulated customer workflow for a joint paid validation.
Fallback ask: define an integration boundary and introduce the customer owner.

## Close

“This sounds specific enough to validate. We can complete one sanitized workflow in two weeks for $4,500, with $2,250 at kickoff. If you are not the buyer, who owns this problem and would you be comfortable introducing us?”

## Exit criteria

A successful conversation ends with at least one of:

- a paid design-partnership owner and contracting step;
- a scheduled technical scoping meeting;
- a named customer introduction;
- a clear disqualification reason recorded in the campaign log.
