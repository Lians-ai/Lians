## Working hypothesis

AgentV evaluates whether an agent is correct, safe, fast, and cost-effective.
Lians controls the historical evidence boundary for the run:

- which facts and policy versions existed at the cutoff;
- which permissions and tools were available;
- which evidence was not yet knowable;
- what changed between two runs; and
- which downstream evaluations depend on a changed source.

The test is commercially meaningful only if AgentV or a customer needs this
historical evidence strongly enough to fund it.

3. Compare behavior and evidence quality.
4. Produce one verifiable evidence receipt per run.
5. Decide whether a production provider, grader, example, or separate
