# Lians outreach cadence

## Touch 1 — evidence hypothesis

Personalize one consequential workflow and ask for a 20-minute evidence-gap
review. Do not attach files.

## Touch 2 — concrete artifact

Send four business days later if there is no reply. Include one precise
question and link or attach the two-week sprint scope.

> Could your team reproduce which source, policy, permission, and tool result
> were valid when one selected agent action occurred? I attached the fixed
> two-week scope so you can see the exact deliverable and $4,500 fee.

## Touch 3 — close the loop

Send five business days after Touch 2 if there is no reply.

> I’ll close the loop after this note. If point-in-time evidence for AI actions
> is on your roadmap, reply with one workflow and I’ll return a one-page gap
> hypothesis. If it is not relevant, no response is needed.

## Reply handling

- **Interested:** schedule discovery and send the scope the same day.
- **Needs routing:** thank the sender and continue in the same thread with the
  referred person.
- **Wants free pilot:** decline unpaid implementation. A short qualification
  call may be complimentary, but every design partnership, integration,
  analysis, and customer-specific deliverable is paid. Hold the standard
  two-week engagement at $4,500.
- **Security concern:** propose synthetic data and no production credentials.
- **Budget objection:** first reduce scope without reducing price. Only if the
  buyer cannot authorize $4,500, offer a separately signed $2,500 one-week
  paid assessment with no integration, credited toward the full partnership.
- **Not now:** ask for a specific quarter and set one follow-up.
- **No:** close the thread immediately.

## Exit rule

Stop after three relevant touches or any opt-out. Never continue after a clear
decline.
