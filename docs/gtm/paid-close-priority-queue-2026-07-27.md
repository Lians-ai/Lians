## Meeting qualification rule

A meeting advances only if it produces all of the following:

1. one consequential AI workflow;
2. one named owner who can approve spend;
3. confirmation that a paid engagement is possible;
4. one success criterion;
5. a dated decision or contracting action.

If an account offers only technical feedback, cloud credits, a partner portal, or vague future interest, retain it as ecosystem infrastructure but remove it from the active paid-close queue.

## Close sequence

1. Confirm the workflow and approver.
2. Restate the standard two-week scope and $4,500 fixed fee.
3. Send the order form only after verbal or written commercial acceptance.
4. Require signature and cleared $2,250 kickoff payment before work begins.
5. Do not route payment through the visible OnePile Stripe account without explicit authorization for Lians.
