## Working hypothesis

Trade AI Edge helps users evaluate market information and investment claims.
Lians can preserve the exact filings, market data, research, model state,
policies, prompts, tools, and approvals behind a generated claim or action. If
any source changes, Lians distinguishes what was available at decision time and
shows which later analyses or user-facing outputs depend on it.

The value is not another trading signal. It is defensible historical evidence
for how a consequential financial conclusion was produced.

## Proposed sanitized workflow

1. A synthetic company event or filing triggers an AI-generated claim.
2. The workflow retrieves public filings, market data, and internal rules.
3. The system produces an analysis or customer-facing conclusion.
4. A cited source, extraction, model, or policy later changes.
5. Lians reconstructs the original evidence state, flags evidence-quality
   problems, and maps affected downstream outputs.

No customer portfolio data, brokerage credentials, or live trading action is
required.
