## The question we answer

If this AI decision is challenged after its data, memory, or policies change,
can the organization prove exactly what the system knew, relied on, and was
allowed to do at that moment?

## Customer provides

- One representative OpenTelemetry trace or reproducible test workflow
- A technical owner for two working sessions
- Synthetic or sanitized examples of relevant memory, retrieved sources,
  policies, permissions, tool responses, and approvals
- A description of the decision or action that must be reviewable

No production credentials or unrestricted customer data are required.

## Lians delivers

1. A working source-system or OpenTelemetry-to-Lians evidence path for the
   selected workflow
2. Trace-to-decision correlation
3. Point-in-time reconstruction of available memory and sources
4. Policy, permission, tool-result, and approval capture where applicable
5. Detection of missing sources, conflicting or stale memories, broken
   references, and incomplete evidence
6. One independently verifiable evidence receipt
7. A concise gap map and production recommendation
8. A recorded delivery walkthrough

## Acceptance criteria

The sprint is complete when the customer can:

- select the agreed decision from its trace;
- identify the exact evidence available at decision time;
- distinguish later or superseded facts from contemporaneous evidence;
- see any material capture gaps;
- export and verify the resulting receipt; and
- receive a written recommendation for production adoption.

## Boundaries

The sprint does not include production rollout, regulatory certification,
legal advice, custom model development, or unlimited integrations. Any handling
of sensitive production data requires a separate security review and written
agreement.
