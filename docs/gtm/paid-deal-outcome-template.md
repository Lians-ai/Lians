## Decision

Choose exactly one:

- **Won:** all seven gates confirmed, including cleared payment.
- **Contracting:** written commercial acceptance exists; signature or payment is
  still outstanding.
- **Qualified:** workflow, owner, and budget path exist; commercial acceptance
  does not.
- **Referral route:** no internal budget; a named customer introduction was made.
- **Nurture:** real problem, but no current owner or budget.
- **Disqualified:** no consequential workflow, duplicated capability, no budget,
  or refusal to pay.

## Follow-up rule

Do not write “great call,” “strong interest,” “partnership,” or “likely buyer” as
a status. Record the named workflow, owner, budget, document, invoice, or payment
evidence instead.
