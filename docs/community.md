# Join the Lians developer community

The fastest way to understand Lians is to reproduce a failure, inspect the
receipt, and try to break the temporal boundary.

## Start in ten minutes

1. Run the [lookahead-bias demo](../demo/lookahead-bias).
2. Inspect the generated receipts for memories retrieved from the future.
3. Change one assumption or replace Lians with your own retrieval layer.
4. Share the result in [Show and tell](https://github.com/Lians-ai/Lians/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).

Useful results include failures. A small counterexample is more valuable than
a broad claim that nobody else can reproduce.

## Where to participate

- **Questions:** use [Q&A](https://github.com/Lians-ai/Lians/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Ideas:** propose the developer problem in [Ideas](https://github.com/Lians-ai/Lians/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Reproducible bugs:** use the [issue forms](https://github.com/Lians-ai/Lians/issues/new/choose).
- **Security reports:** follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Code contributions:** read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Maintainer response standard

Maintainers aim to acknowledge complete community reports within two business
days. Complex fixes may take longer, but reporters should know whether a result
was reproduced, needs more evidence, or is outside the project's current scope.

## Community principles

- Reproducible evidence beats promotional claims.
- Synthetic fixtures are preferred when real data is sensitive or licensed.
- Benchmarks must state models, prompts, data boundaries, and limitations.
- No purchased stars, reciprocal-star campaigns, or manufactured engagement.
- Criticism backed by a minimal reproduction is a contribution.

The current contribution themes are listed in the [public roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
