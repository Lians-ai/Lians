# Pricing experiment

These are test prices, not public commitments.

## Offers

| Offer | Test price | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Local | Free | One device, local storage, inspect/correct/delete |
| Pro design partner | $12/month or $99/year | Encrypted cross-device continuity, backup, priority setup support |
| Team pilot | $99/month for five seats | Shared project memory, roles, administration, and direct support |

Do not charge for technical-preview continuity as if it were generally
available. Early payments must be framed as bounded design-partner pilots with a
clear refund and cancellation path.

## Test sequence

1. Ask activated users whether they would pay without showing a price.
2. Show one randomly assigned Pro price: $9, $12, or $15 per month.
3. Measure checkout intent separately from completed payment.
4. Interview every paying design partner after two weeks.
5. Keep a price only when conversion, 30-day retention, support load, and gross
   margin all remain acceptable.

## First validation target

At 1,000 activated users, aim for either 50 Pro customers or ten team pilots.
That validates willingness to pay; it does not by itself prove a durable,
profitable company.
